Drop the score in this folder named:  score.mp3

Also accepted: score.m4a, score.wav, score.ogg

The player control appears under the "Soundtrack" heading only when
one of those files is present. If the folder is empty the control
stays hidden and the page is unaffected.

MP3 or M4A recommended - they play everywhere and stay small.
Keep it under ~10MB so it starts quickly.
